Dr. Xu Zheng studied engineering at Northeastern University and earned a PhD in artificial intelligence at HKUST. Research at INSAIT and Queen Mary University of London, together with work at Soul AI Lab, Tavus, and Familiar, shaped his focus on multimodal systems and real-time interaction. He now leads X Lab at Great Bay University, advancing Multimodal and Situational Intelligence.
