GBU PATHWAYS

Application Pathways

Pathway information checked on 6 November 2026. Official Chinese-language notices and formal offer or admission documents take precedence; listed postdoctoral pay is indicative, not an X Lab offer.

01 / APPOINTMENT

Postdoctoral Researchers

  1. GBU operates a national postdoctoral research station; appointments are normally for two years.
  2. Applicants should hold, or expect to obtain, a relevant PhD; candidates are normally within three years of the degree and no more than 35 years old.
  3. Recent 2026 GBU postings have listed indicative comprehensive annual packages of RMB 400,000–450,000 by category, and at least RMB 600,000 for eligible Guangdong overseas postdoctoral talent programme participants. Check the current call for applicable terms.
  4. Official postings additionally list social insurance, housing provident fund contributions, and a rental subsidy. Final terms depend on current policy, eligibility review, and the formal offer.

Official source: GBU Recruitment

02 / DEGREE · PHD

Jointly Trained PhD Students

  1. GBU currently trains doctoral students through joint programmes with partner universities.
  2. Student registration, degree conferral, programme length, funding, and eligibility depend on the specific annual programme.
  3. Contact the PI to discuss research fit before completing the formal application required by GBU and the partner university.

Official sources: Doctoral programmes · 2027 GBU–HIT notice

03 / DEGREE · MASTER

Jointly Trained Master’s Students

  1. Master’s students are currently trained through joint programmes with partner universities.
  2. Disciplines, quotas, student registration, selection, funding, and degree requirements follow the current annual programme notice.
  3. The 2027 GBU–SUSTech joint master’s notice includes Computer Science and Technology and Electronic Information pathways involving SCIT; admission and quotas follow the official notice.

Official sources: Master’s programmes · 2027 GBU–SUSTech notice

04 / IMPORTANT NOTE

Formal Routes & Current Status

  1. Emailing X Lab starts a research-fit conversation; it does not replace a formal appointment or degree application.
  2. GBU is currently a project-construction institution for master’s and doctoral degree authorization; graduate recruitment is conducted through current joint-training programmes.
  3. The 2027 GBU–HIT autumn doctoral call cited here closed on 7 October 2026. Check the latest official notices for other or later rounds.

Official sources: About GBU · Recruitment portal
